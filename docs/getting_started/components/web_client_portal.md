## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What information does the Client Dashboard show?**
A: The Client Dashboard provides an overview of client information, infrastructure status, and scheduler pack health — giving you a quick snapshot of overall system performance.

**Q: What container operations can I perform from the Client Portal?**
A: You can start, stop, restart, and view logs for containers directly from the Client Portal. You can also monitor pod CPU and memory usage for infrastructure health tracking.

**Q: Who should use the Client Portal?**
A: The Client Portal is designed for administrators and operators who need to monitor client infrastructure health and manage container lifecycles from a centralized interface.
