# EasyTask Support

## Need Help?

We're here to assist you with EasyTask questions and issues.

## Getting Support

### Quick Links
- **Official Website**: [runeasytask.io](https://runeasytask.io)
- **Documentation**: [runeasytask.io/docs](https://runeasytask.io/docs)

### Contact Support
For direct support, visit our landing page and use the "Contact Support" button:
**[runeasytask.io](https://runeasytask.io)**

### Self-Service Resources
- Check existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Evolve-Software-LLC/easytask/issues) for solutions
- Browse comprehensive documentation at runeasytask.io/docs

### Before Asking
1. Check the [documentation](https://runeasytask.io/docs)
2. Search existing [issues](https://github.com/Evolve-Software-LLC/easytask/issues)
3. Review the [beginner's guide](https://runeasytask.io/docs/beginner-guide/)

### Reporting Bugs
When reporting bugs, please include:
- EasyTask version
- Operating system and environment
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected vs actual behavior
- Relevant logs or screenshots
